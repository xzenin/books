Split the write.py features into 5 crew agent 
1) BookLayout (it will create master plan for the book) 
    - The Hook: Start as close to the action as possible.
    - The Goal: Remind the reader what the character wants right now.
    - The Conflict: Introduce obstacles that prevent the goal.
    - The Reaction: The character processes the disaster (Rationalize).
    - The Dilemma: They realize they have no easy choices left.
    - The Decision: They pick a new Goal, which starts the next chapter.

2) RefinetChapter (It will put additional context to book a) randomize thoughts and optional human in the loop 
    - Goal: Is it clear what the character wants in this chapter?
    = Conflict: Is the obstacle actually difficult, or is it too easy to solve?
    - Logic: Did anything happen that contradicts an earlier chapter?
    - Sensory: Did I include at least two senses other than sight?
    - Ending: Is there a "hook" that leads into the next chapter's Rationalization?

3) LayoutChapter ( It will create multiple segment of the story for the chapter.
    - Goal: What do they want?
    - Stakes: What happens if they fail? (Adds Worry)
    - Vulnerability: Why is this hard for them specifically? (Adds Connection)
    - Conflict: What stands in their way? (Adds Friction)
    - Tension: Why must they do it now? (Adds Speed)
    - Rationalize: How do they process the failure and pivot? (Adds Logic)
    - Subversion: What unexpected thing just changed the game? (Adds Surprise)
    - Catharsis: What is the final emotional release? (Adds Satisfaction) 
4) DraftChapterSegment (It will actually create self independent prompt and save it) 
    - Chapter Mission: Escape the city before the gates close.
    - Segment 1 Prep:
        - Goal: Get to the horse stables.
        - Conflict: The main road is blocked by guards.
        - Therefore: He must take the muddy back alleys.
    - Segment 2 Prep:
        - Carry-over: He is covered in mud and frustrated (Emotional temperature).
        - Goal: Sneak past the stable boy.
        - Conflict: He trips and makes a noise.
    - But: Instead of getting caught, he realizes the stable boy is asleep.
    - Segment 3 Prep:
        - Pivot: He has the horse, but now he hears the gate bells ringing (Tension/Ticking clock).
        - Goal: Race to the gate before it shuts.
5) GenerateChapterSegment (It will actually create the content for a segment based on the prompt created in previous step) 
    - Generate content per segement. Add to the chapter_text array.
    - Physical State: What is the character holding? Are they injured? Where are they standing?
    - Emotional State: Is the character feeling more desperate or more confident than they were 5 minutes ago?
    - Information State: What does the character know now that they didn't know at the start of this segment?
